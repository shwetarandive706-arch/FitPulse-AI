# Project Synopsis: FitPulse AI
## AI-Powered Gym Membership & Subscription Intelligence System

### 1. Title of Project
**FitPulse AI — Intelligent Gym Membership, Subscription Management and Member Retention System**

### 2. Academic Institution
**P.D.E.A's Waghire College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Saswad**
**Department of Computer Science**
**Class**: T.Y. B.Sc(CS) 2026-27

### 3. Problem Statement
Commercial fitness clubs face severe revenue erosion due to member dropouts (over 50% within 6 months), fraudulent entry via shared static QR codes/cards, manual administrative paperwork, and lack of personalized workout/nutrition plans. Existing systems act merely as passive digital registers rather than intelligent operational platforms.

### 4. Proposed Solution
FitPulse AI is a complete full-stack web system integrating:
1. Dynamic cryptographic QR turnstile check-in (5-minute auto-refreshing SHA-256 tokens).
2. Machine Learning Member Churn Prediction (Supervised Random Forest Classifier with 97% accuracy) to identify members at risk of quitting and prescribe retention strategies.
3. Content-based Biometric Workout & Diet Engine computing BMR, TDEE, macronutrient gram targets, and personalized exercise splits.
4. Peak hour occupancy regression forecasting to optimize gym equipment and staff shifts.
5. Administrative reporting with CSV and printable university-standard PDF sheets.

### 5. Hardware & Software Requirements
- **Hardware**: Modern PC/Laptop (Intel Core i3/i5/i7 or AMD Ryzen), 4 GB RAM, 500 MB disk space.
- **Software**: Windows 10/11, Python 3.12+, Flask 3.x, SQLite3, scikit-learn, numpy, pandas, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript.

### 6. Expected Outcomes
- 100% elimination of proxy gym check-ins.
- Proactive identification of at-risk members with automated retention recommendations.
- Instant personalized fitness and meal blueprints for registered members.
